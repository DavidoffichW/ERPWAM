# Historical draft — ERPWAM v0.2.2

These notes describe qualified planning-interface work that was not published as a tagged release. They are retained for repository history and are not release notes for the analytical redesign.

ERPWAM now presents its planning tools as a compact operational workspace. The planning grid, replacement candidates, portfolio constraints and results, and decision history take priority; mappings, validation findings, Priority Lists, metric definitions, and detailed evidence remain available when needed.

The release also incorporates the previously qualified v0.1.1 engineering work: independent domain, workbook, persistence, browser, and offline tests; stricter ranking-population validation; corrected bounded portfolio selection; and safer service-worker updates.

## Highlights

- Task-first Planning Data, Recommendations, Portfolio, History, and Overview views.
- Accessible icon controls, concise contextual help, predictable keyboard focus, and responsive dark and light themes.
- Manual replacement selection remains first-class, including permitted records with advisory warnings.
- Exact and approximate portfolio results are clearly distinguished.
- Existing Excel, evidence, browser-storage, backup, migration, and offline contracts remain compatible.

ERPWAM remains a browser-local decision-support workbench. Its rankings and synthetic model are demonstrations, not operational policy or trained field inference.
